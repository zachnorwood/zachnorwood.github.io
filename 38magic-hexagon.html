The magic hexagon

The numbers 1 through 19 arranged so that every straight line of cells sums to 38. Click a cell to see a line through it, and click it again to turn to the next direction.

Click any cell

The cells hold 1 + 2 + … + 19 = 190 in total, and the five parallel rows split that evenly, so each line must sum to 190/5 = 38. Clifford Adams found this arrangement in the 1950s after decades of searching. Up to rotation and reflection, it is the only normal magic hexagon besides the trivial one-cell hexagon.
